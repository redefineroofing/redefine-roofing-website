Premium Roofs, Two Approvals

Lakewood Ranch's villages — from Summerfield to Del Webb to the neighborhoods near Waterside Place — run newer and nicer, with strict architectural standards and often tile or designer-profile shingle roofs. A replacement here has to pass the architectural review board and the county inspector, and we manage both: ARB color and material submissions up front, then a complete Atlas Signature Select system (or matched tile) installed to exceed Florida wind code.

Lakewood Ranch Roof Replacement: What Homeowners Ask

Do you handle the architectural review board process in Lakewood Ranch?

Completely — it's step one of every project. We prepare color samples against your village's approved palette, product specs, and the application, and we don't schedule tear-off until approval is issued.

Can you replace tile roofs, not just shingle?

Yes. We install designer-profile architectural shingle systems and tile, matched to your home and the ARB's requirements.

Will a new roof lower my Lakewood Ranch insurance?

Usually meaningfully. A documented replacement resets the age question, and sealed decks and enhanced nailing qualify for wind-mitigation credits. We document everything your inspector needs.

Which villages do you serve?

All of them — Summerfield, Greenbrook, Country Club, Del Webb, Esplanade, the Waterside-area neighborhoods, on both the Manatee and Sarasota county sides.

Do you install tile roofs in Lakewood Ranch?

Yes. Many Lakewood Ranch villages favor tile or designer-profile architectural shingle, and we install both, matched to your home and the architectural review board's requirements. Tile replacement includes proper underlayment and correctly set, matching pieces — not shortcuts that fail early.
