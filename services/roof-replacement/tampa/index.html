A Complete System, Not Just New Shingles

Tampa's housing runs from craftsman bungalows in Seminole Heights and Hyde Park to two-stories in Westchase and New Tampa, and a real replacement treats the whole roof as one engineered system regardless of the home's age — decking inspection, underlayment, flashing, ventilation, and shingles that work together. As an Atlas Manufacturer Certified installer, we build the complete Atlas Signature Select system, rated to exceed the Florida Building Code's wind requirements for this region. Because we don't mix components across manufacturers, your full material warranty stays intact.

Older Tampa Homes Hide Surprises

Under a historic Tampa roof you often find plank decking, layered tear-offs, and framing that predates modern code. We tear off completely, inspect and repair the decking before anything new goes on, and match shingle profiles and colors to the neighborhood so a new roof doesn't fight a century-old street. Inside city limits, replacement permits run through the City of Tampa and we pull and manage them; unincorporated areas go through Hillsborough County.

What a Tampa Replacement Includes

  • Full tear-off and decking inspection, with rotted wood replaced
  • Synthetic underlayment, new drip edge, and proper flashing at every penetration
  • Balanced attic ventilation corrected while the roof is open
  • Atlas Signature Select shingles installed with enhanced nailing
  • Full cleanup with a magnetic nail sweep and a final walkthrough

Signs Your Tampa Roof May Be Ready to Replace

Across Tampa's range of homes, the signs a roof is ready to replace vary — but these come up most:

  • Shingles past 15-20 years — Tampa's sun ages them faster than the national average
  • Repeated leaks in different spots after years of patching
  • Granules filling the gutters and bald, blackened shingle patches
  • A roof age your insurance carrier is now questioning at renewal

Not sure which side of the line your roof is on? Our free, no-pressure inspection gives you a written, photographed answer — and if a repair still serves a sound roof, we'll tell you.

Roof Replacement in Tampa: Common Questions

Do I need a City of Tampa permit to replace my roof?

Yes. Replacements inside Tampa city limits permit through the City of Tampa; unincorporated areas go through Hillsborough County. Either way, as your licensed contractor we pull the permit and schedule the required inspections.

Can you replace a historic Tampa bungalow roof without ruining its character?

Yes — it's a specialty. We match shingle profile and color to the street, preserve detail at eaves and gables, and repair the older decking underneath properly. Seminole Heights, Hyde Park, and Ybor-area homes are a regular part of our Tampa work.

How long does a roof replacement take in Tampa?

Most residential replacements take one to three days of on-site work once permits and materials are ready. Complex historic roofs or homes needing significant decking repair can take longer — we give a realistic timeline in your written quote.

Will a new roof help with my Tampa homeowners insurance?

Often. Florida carriers offer wind-mitigation credits for roofs built to current code with sealed decks and proper nailing, and a newer roof is easier to insure. We document what your wind-mitigation inspector needs to capture available discounts.

What roofing materials do you install on Tampa replacements?

Primarily the complete Atlas Signature Select architectural shingle system, which we're certified to install, plus metal roofing — standing seam, exposed fastener, and stone-coated steel — when a Tampa homeowner wants it. We'll walk through the options, costs, and trade-offs for your specific home during the free estimate, with no pressure toward the priciest choice.
