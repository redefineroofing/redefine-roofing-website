Builder-Grade Roofs Meet Real Storms

Riverview's fast-built communities — Panther Trace, Triple Creek, Summerfield — went up with minimum-spec roofs that summer hail and thunderstorm wind punish. After a storm our crews respond 24/7 with emergency tarping, then inspect closely, because entry-level shingles bruise and shed granules in ways an insurer will try to call "aging." We document the storm's fingerprint with dated photos before any repair.

Claims and HOA Approval, Both Handled

We work the claim for you — documenting to an adjuster's standard, meeting the adjuster on-site, and advocating for legitimate coverage. If storm damage means visible repairs or a section replacement, we also prepare the HOA color and material approval your community requires. Permits run through Hillsborough County, pulled by us. Florida's tight filing window means acting promptly.

Storm Damage Signs on a Riverview Roof

  • Bruised, soft shingles and stripped granules from hail
  • Dented gutters, vents, and flashing
  • Wind-lifted or creased shingles on windward slopes
  • Displaced ridge caps and exposed nails
  • Fresh interior stains after the storm

If it's wear, not storm, we'll be honest. Licensed and insured, CCC 1331457.

Recent Storms and What They Left Behind

Riverview's storm damage tends to arrive with the fierce summer thunderstorms that roll across southern Hillsborough County, plus the tornado threat systems like Milton brought to the region in 2024. On builder-grade roofs barely a decade old, that weather does more than its share of damage — and because the community's roofs are so uniform, we're careful to document each home individually rather than assume the whole street fared alike.

Riverview Storm Damage & Claims: Questions Answered

Do you handle HOA approval for storm repairs in Riverview?

Yes. If storm repairs are visible or involve replacing a section, we prepare the color samples and documentation Panther Trace, Triple Creek, Summerfield, and the other communities require.

Will insurance cover hail damage on my newer Riverview roof?

If hail caused genuine damage, it can — even on a newer roof. Builder-grade shingles bruise and lose granules under hail, and our dated documentation proves the storm's role rather than letting it be dismissed as wear.

Do you tarp Riverview roofs after a storm?

Yes, 24/7. Emergency tarping protects your interior while the claim and permanent repair are arranged.

A contractor offered to cover my deductible after the storm. Legit?

No — waiving a homeowner's deductible is illegal in Florida and a storm-chaser red flag. We document real damage and follow the law. Verify any contractor's Florida license and local address first.

Which Riverview communities do you serve for storm damage?

All of them — Panther Trace, Summerfield, Triple Creek, Winthrop, South Fork, Rivercrest, and the areas along Big Bend Road and US-301.
