Clearwater Storm Damage & Claims: What to Know

Do you provide 24/7 emergency roof service on Clearwater Beach?

Yes — beach, Island Estates, and mainland alike. When a Gulf storm opens your roof, we respond around the clock with emergency tarping to stop interior damage before it spreads.

Will the insurance company blame my coastal roof's age instead of the storm?

It's a common tactic, which is exactly why documentation matters. We record the storm damage to an adjuster's standard, attend the inspection, and make the case that legitimate storm damage is covered rather than dismissed as coastal wear.

How long do I have to file a hurricane claim in Clearwater?

Florida now generally requires it within a year of the date of loss, with a tighter window for supplemental claims. We document your damage immediately so nothing is jeopardized by delay.

Should I trust a contractor who shows up offering free roof inspections after a storm?

Be cautious. Verify a physical local address and a Florida license (ours is CCC 1331457), and never sign with anyone pressuring you on the spot or promising to cover your deductible — that's illegal in Florida.

Can storm damage lead to a full roof replacement claim?

Sometimes, yes. When wind damage is widespread enough that a repair won't restore the roof, a replacement can be the covered outcome. Our documentation supports the honest extent of the damage, whatever it is.
