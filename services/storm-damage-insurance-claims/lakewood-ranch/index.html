Lakewood Ranch Storm & Hail Claims: What Homeowners Ask

Can you repair storm-damaged tile roofs in Lakewood Ranch?

Yes. Tile storm repair requires matching profile and color and setting pieces correctly over sound underlayment — not a quick patch. We source matching tile and repair to the standard the ARB and your insurer expect.

Do you handle ARB approval for storm repairs?

Yes. For any visible repair or section replacement, we prepare the color and material documentation your Lakewood Ranch village's architectural review board requires.

How do I know if hail damaged my Lakewood Ranch roof?

Hail damage is often subtle — cracked tiles, bruised shingles, dented flashing and gutters. Our inspection documents it with photos, which is what a hail claim requires.

Do you offer emergency tarping in Lakewood Ranch?

Yes, 24/7, whenever a storm leaves open damage. We protect the interior first, then work the claim and permanent repair.

How long do I have to file a storm claim?

Florida generally requires storm claims within a year of the date of loss. We document immediately so your claim is filed well within the deadline.
