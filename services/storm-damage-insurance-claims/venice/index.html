After a Venice Storm, Look For

  • Whole sections of shingles lifted or gone
  • Corroded coastal flashing peeled on island homes
  • Displaced ridge caps and exposed fasteners
  • Granule loss and dented soft metals
  • Interior stains and soffit damage

Venice Hurricane & Storm Claims: What to Know

Do you provide emergency roof service on Venice Island after a hurricane?

Yes — island and mainland, 24/7. When a storm opens your roof, we respond around the clock with emergency tarping to stop interior damage before it spreads.

The claim process is overwhelming. Will you actually guide me through it?

Yes. We take the time to explain each step, document the damage to an adjuster's standard, meet the adjuster at your home, and advocate for legitimate coverage — no pressure, no jargon dumped on you.

How long do I have to file a hurricane claim in Venice?

Florida now generally requires it within a year of the date of loss, with a tighter window for supplemental claims. We document immediately so nothing is lost to delay.

After a hurricane, crews flood in offering fast roofs. How do I choose safely?

Verify a Florida license (ours is CCC 1331457) and a permanent local address, insist on a written scope, and refuse anyone who pressures a same-day signature or offers to cover your deductible — that's illegal in Florida.

Can hurricane damage lead to a full replacement claim?

Often, yes. When wind damage is widespread enough that a repair won't restore the roof, replacement can be the covered outcome, and our documentation supports the true extent of the damage.
