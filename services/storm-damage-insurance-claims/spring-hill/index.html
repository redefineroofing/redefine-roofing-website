Spring Hill sits inland enough that summer supercells drop hail and hard straight-line wind, and because the community was built in one great wave off Spring Hill Drive and Mariner Boulevard, whole streets of similarly-aged ranch roofs take the hit together. Documenting Storm vs. Age

On a same-age Spring Hill roof, the claim turns on separating genuine storm damage from ordinary wear, and careful dated documentation is what wins it. We photograph to an adjuster's standard, meet the adjuster on-site, and advocate for legitimate coverage. Hernando County permits are pulled by us. Spring Hill Storm Damage & Claims: Common Questions

How do I tell hail damage from normal aging on my Spring Hill roof?

Hail leaves a specific fingerprint — bruised shingles, granules stripped in impact patterns, and dented soft metals like gutters and vents. Our inspection documents that pattern with photos, which is what separates a storm claim from a wear denial.

Will insurance cover storm damage on my older Spring Hill roof?

If the storm caused genuine damage, it can — but insurers scrutinize older, same-age roofs hard. Dated documentation distinguishing storm damage from age is what makes the case, and it's what we provide.

Do you offer emergency tarping in Spring Hill?

Yes, 24/7. Emergency tarping protects your interior while the claim and permanent repair are arranged.

How soon should I file after a Spring Hill storm?

Promptly — Florida generally requires storm claims within a year of the date of loss. We document immediately so your claim is filed well within the window.

A crew came through the neighborhood after the storm. Should I sign with them?

Be cautious when crews sweep same-age neighborhoods after a storm. Verify a Florida license and permanent local address, and never sign with anyone offering to cover your deductible — that's illegal in Florida. We're licensed CCC 1331457.
