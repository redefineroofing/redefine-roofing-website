North Port Knows the Difference Now

When Hurricane Ian tore through in 2022, North Port took some of the worst of it, from Warm Mineral Springs to the newest streets of Wellen Park — and the chaotic repair rush that followed taught this town to tell a legitimate roofer from a storm-chasing one. We're Florida licensed since 2015 (CCC 1331457 — verify it), physically headquartered in Tampa Bay, and here for warranty service long after the storm-chasers have moved on.

Two Kinds of Repairs We Make Here

North Port repairs split two ways. First, ordinary storm and age damage — wind-lifted shingles, failed flashing and boots, worn valleys — which we trace to the source and fix with our lifetime workmanship warranty. Second, and increasingly, corrections to rushed post-Ian work: nail pops, improperly sealed penetrations, and flashing shortcuts from crews that installed fast and vanished. If your roof was done in the gold rush and something's off, we'll document exactly what and fix it.

Documented, Honest, and Local

Nobody in North Port needs convincing that documentation matters. City of North Port permits handled. When a roof is genuinely done, we'll walk through a replacement built for the next storm.

North Port Roof Repair: What to Know

My North Port roof was replaced after Ian and now something seems off. Can you check it?

Yes — this is a common call. The post-Ian rush produced plenty of rushed work: nail pops, poorly sealed penetrations, flashing shortcuts. We document the installation's actual condition and repair what's wrong, so a young roof doesn't fail early.

How do I know a roofer is legitimate before I sign?

Verify the license at myfloridalicense.com (ours is CCC 1331457), confirm a physical local address with years of history, and walk away from anyone pressuring same-day signatures or offering to 'cover' your deductible. North Port learned these lessons expensively.

Is North Port really storm-exposed? We're not on the coast.

Ian answered that permanently — inland position moderates surge, not wind. North Port sits in the path of Gulf systems crossing the peninsula, so we build and repair accordingly.

Do you serve Wellen Park?

Yes — Wellen Park's villages are full members of our North Port coverage, HOA documentation included, along with all of North Port from Warm Mineral Springs to the county line.
