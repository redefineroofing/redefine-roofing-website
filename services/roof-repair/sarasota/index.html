Low-Slope Is Where Sarasota Roofs Leak

Sarasota takes its architecture seriously — this is the home of the Sarasota School of Architecture, and neighborhoods like Gulf Gate and Arlington Park are full of mid-century homes with dramatic low-slope and flat rooflines. Those flat sections are exactly where these roofs leak, and they're where a shingle-only crew fails by stretching shingles past their rated pitch. We repair low-slope sections with proper membrane systems, integrated cleanly with any shingle field — so the water management works as well as the sightlines.

Repairs That Respect the Design

On a Sarasota home where the roofline is the architecture, a clumsy patch is a visible mistake. We detail repairs to preserve the horizontal lines these homes are built around, match materials carefully, and handle the transitions and penetrations where flat and shingle meet. Every repair carries our lifetime workmanship warranty.

Coastal Exposure Under the Aesthetics

Bayfront and barrier-adjacent Sarasota lives with salt-influenced air and open-water wind, and recent hurricane seasons drove the point home countywide. After a storm, our storm team documents damage and works your carrier; for the sale-and-insurance market, our free inspections produce reports buyers and carriers accept. Sarasota Roof Repair: Common Questions

My mid-century Sarasota home has a flat roof that leaks. Can you fix it right?

Yes — low-slope and flat sections are a Sarasota specialty for us. They need proper membrane repair, not shingles stretched past their rated slope, and clean detailing where they meet any pitched roof. Done right, the leak stops recurring.

Will a repair ruin the clean lines of my Sarasota School-style home?

Not the way we do it. We detail repairs to preserve the horizontal rooflines these homes are built around and match materials carefully, so the fix reads as part of the architecture rather than a patch.

Do you repair roofs on Sarasota's bayfront and barrier-adjacent homes?

Yes — those homes get coastal-appropriate components and wind detailing suited to their open-water exposure.

Can you provide a repair report for a Sarasota home sale?

Yes. Our free inspection produces a written, photographed report on condition and remaining life that buyers, sellers, and insurers all accept — useful whether you're listing or negotiating.
