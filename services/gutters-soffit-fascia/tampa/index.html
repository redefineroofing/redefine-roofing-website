Oak Canopy Meets Bungalow Woodwork

The grand oaks that make Seminole Heights, Hyde Park, and South Tampa beautiful also drop leaves and debris that pack gutters solid, and the older bungalows below them wear real wood fascia that Tampa humidity rots the moment water backs up. We install seamless aluminum gutters sized for Tampa's downpours, add gutter guards where the canopy demands it, and repair or replace rotted fascia and soffit with materials matched to a historic home's character.

Why Soffit and Fascia Aren't Optional

Fascia carries your gutters and closes the roof edge; soffit ventilates the attic and keeps pests out. When either fails — and in Tampa's climate they fail from the back, through moisture — the damage spreads into the roof deck and attic before you ever see it from the street. We catch it at the edge. Inside city limits, permits run through the City of Tampa where required, handled by us.

Why It Pays to Get the Roof Edge Right in Tampa

There's a resale angle too: in Tampa's competitive historic-district market, buyers and their inspectors notice a rotting fascia board or sagging gutter immediately, reading it as deferred maintenance that raises questions about the roof itself. Tampa Gutters, Soffit & Fascia: Common Questions

Do I need gutter guards under Tampa's oak trees?

If oaks overhang your roof, they help enormously. Tampa's canopy packs gutters with leaves and debris fast, and quality guards cut the cleaning burden and prevent the overflow that rots fascia. We size the guard to your specific tree load.

My Tampa bungalow's wood fascia is soft and peeling. Can you fix it?

Yes — soft, peeling fascia is rot, usually from gutters that overflowed or backed up. We remove the damaged wood, address the moisture source, and replace it with material matched to your bungalow, so the repair looks original rather than patched.

What does soffit actually do?

Soffit is the underside of your roof overhang. It ventilates the attic by drawing in intake air, and it seals the roof edge against birds, squirrels, and insects. Damaged soffit means both an attic-heat problem and an open door for pests.

Do you match gutter colors to my house?

Yes. Our seamless aluminum gutters come in a wide color range, formed on-site to your roofline, so they complement the home rather than stand out.

Can you replace gutters and fascia when I get a new roof?

Absolutely, and it's the ideal time — the roof edge is already open and accessible. Coordinating gutter, fascia, and soffit work with a reroof saves labor and gives you a fully sound roof edge in one project.
