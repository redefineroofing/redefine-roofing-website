Drainage That Respects the Design

Sarasota's design heritage — the low, horizontal lines of Gulf Gate and the Sarasota School tradition — means gutters have to handle heavy rain without cluttering a clean roofline. We install discreet, color-matched seamless gutters routed to preserve the architecture, using coastal-appropriate materials for bayfront and barrier-adjacent homes where salt attacks standard hardware. Where a design calls for it, we work with concealed and low-profile approaches.

Fascia and Soffit as Part of the Whole

On a distinctive Sarasota home, a rotted fascia board or sagging soffit disrupts the line the architect intended — and exposes the roof edge and attic. We repair and match them precisely, restore soffit ventilation, and seal against pests, treating the edge as part of the home's design rather than an afterthought. City of Sarasota or Sarasota County permits handled where required.

Why It Pays to Get the Roof Edge Right in Sarasota

In Sarasota's design-conscious market, the roof edge is part of the architecture's language, and a mismatched gutter or rotted fascia reads as loudly to a knowledgeable buyer as a bad addition would. Treating drainage and the roof edge as design elements — discreet, matched, and coastal-durable — protects both the home's structure and the architectural integrity that gives these homes their value.

Sarasota Gutters, Soffit & Fascia: Common Questions

Can gutters be installed without ruining my mid-century home's lines?

Yes — that's how we approach Sarasota work. Discreet, color-matched seamless gutters routed thoughtfully handle the rain while preserving the clean horizontal lines these homes were designed around.

Do bayfront Sarasota homes need special gutter materials?

They benefit from coastal-grade, corrosion-resistant components. Salt air near the bay and barrier islands attacks standard hardware, so exposed homes get a spec built to last.

Will you match the fascia on my architecturally distinctive home?

Yes. We repair or replace fascia to match the profile and finish precisely, so the roof edge reads as part of the original design rather than a patch.

Is soffit repair something you handle?

Yes — we repair soffit, restore its attic ventilation, and seal pest gaps, keeping the underside of the overhang consistent with the home's look.

Can you coordinate with a roof replacement?

Yes, and it's the efficient time — gutters, fascia, and soffit are handled together while the roof edge is open, in one clean project.
